Set up in three steps

  1. 1

    Connect the browser

    Give each child a pairing code, then type it into the Praise extension on their browser. Each code works once and expires after 15 minutes.

  2. 2

    Choose the rules

    Start from Child safe, Teen or Education, then change anything: kinds of sites, domain endings, downloads and cookies.

  3. 3

    Let them browse

    Known sites open straight away. A new site waits for a short check, is classified once, and is remembered after that.

What it does

Closed means closed, and your child knows why

A closed page says what kind of site it is and which rule closed it, in plain words. Your child can tap "Ask a parent to open it", and the request waits on your dashboard for a yes.

Money stays out of reach

Banking, payments, UPI, wallets, trading, crypto and loans are closed by default in Child safe. Look-alike sign-in pages that imitate a bank are flagged as fake, even on a domain nobody has seen before.

  • paypal.com Closed
  • phonepe.com Closed
  • secure-examplebank-login.xyz Fake bank page
  • khanacademy.org Open

Downloads and other browsers, handled

Programs, scripts and installers are stopped, and so are pages for downloading another browser and links that try to hand a page to one. Documents and images go through.

  • ChromeSetup.exe Stopped
  • run-me.bat Stopped
  • essay.pdf Allowed
  • diagram.png Allowed

See what sites keep in the browser

Cookies are listed by name, site, lifetime and likely purpose, with a confidence level, because a name alone can't prove what a cookie does. Known trackers are removed. Praise never reads a cookie's contents.

Your rules come first. The AI only advises.

When a site is new, an AI model suggests a category and a risk score. It never makes the final call. Praise applies the same fixed order every time, so an AI guess can't override something you decided.

A site is classified once and then remembered, so repeat visits are quick and don't need the AI again.

  1. Your own allow, block and approved lists
  2. Sites a reviewer has verified
  3. Known security threats
  4. Your rule for each kind of site
  5. Domain endings you've allowed or blocked
  6. The AI's suggestion, when it's sure enough
  7. Your default for sites nobody has seen

What Praise doesn't do

  • It protects the browser it's installed in. It can't control other browsers or apps on the computer.
  • A child who can open the browser's extensions page can switch Praise off, unless it's installed with a managed browser policy. Praise closes that page, but that slows a determined child down. It isn't a lock.
  • The AI can be wrong. A site it calls safe is trusted only by the family it was found through until a reviewer verifies it, and you can ask for any classification to be reviewed.

Privacy

  • Cookie contents are never collected, only names, sites and lifetimes.
  • Browsing history is deleted after 90 days.
  • The shared site list holds domains and categories, never who visited them.
  • Your rules and activity are kept in your own account, and you can remove a browser at any time.
