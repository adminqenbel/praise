    Four layers, each one yours to adjust

    Safe browsing

    Checks new sites as you open them. A scam page or a look-alike bank site gets a warning with the reason, and the real address when it imitates one. Known malware is blocked outright.

    Trackers and cookies

    Blocks advertising and cross-site trackers, removes tracking codes such as fbclid from links, and clears tracking cookies. A report for each page shows what was stopped.

    Data guard

    Asks before card numbers, ID numbers, one-time codes or passwords go to a flagged page, an unencrypted one, or a form that posts to a different site. It checks on your device and never sees what you typed.

    Password safety

    Tells you when a password has appeared in known breaches, without sending the password anywhere. Optionally warns when you reuse one across sites.

    Honest limits, and who is in control

    What it can't do

    • It only sees the browser it's installed in.
    • A form that sends data with a script, rather than a normal submit, can't be intercepted, and forms inside embedded frames aren't watched.
    • The AI can be wrong. A scam page with no obvious signs can look fine until a reviewer catches it.

    You're in charge

    • Pause it for an hour or until tomorrow from the toolbar.
    • Choose what to warn about, block or clean up, one setting at a time.
    • Continue past any warning except known malware.
    • Browsing history is deleted after 90 days.

    Protect your own browsing

    Create an account, connect your browser with a one-time code, and keep the defaults or change any of them.
